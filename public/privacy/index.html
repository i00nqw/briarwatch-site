Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Briarwatch (for Android, upcoming iOS releases, and the companion website at briarwatch.com) collects, uses, and protects information, and the privacy choices available to you.

Summary

  • Core gameplay and local saves work offline on your device without creating a Briarwatch account.
  • Analytics: We use or will enable Google Analytics (including Google Analytics for Firebase on mobile) to understand gameplay balance, diagnose crashes, and improve app performance.
  • Advertising: The mobile app integrates Google Ads / Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads at natural breaks.
  • Technical data handled by Google: Google analytics and advertising services may process your IP address, IP-derived approximate (coarse) location, device and app identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or, on iOS with your permission, IDFA), app and ad interactions, and diagnostics.
  • Your consent & controls: Where required by law, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) displays ad consent choices before ads are requested, and you can revisit them in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iOS, the app requests App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before accessing the advertising identifier.
  • What we never request in gameplay: We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or home address in gameplay, and we do not access your contacts, photos, personal files, messages, health data, or precise GPS location.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.

1. Scope: Mobile App & Website

This policy applies to the Briarwatch mobile game (Android package com.briarwatch.game and future iOS App Store releases) and the official website at https://briarwatch.com, operated by Mio/i00nqw Game Studios (Ion Iuncu).

The briarwatch.com website is a static informational site. It does not set advertising cookies, run web analytics scripts, or require account registration. The analytics and advertising disclosures below apply to the Briarwatch mobile applications when those services are active.

2. Information Collected

Gameplay & App Analytics (Google Analytics / Firebase)

To fix bugs, monitor stability, and tune difficulty and progression, Briarwatch uses or will enable Google Analytics (including Google Analytics for Firebase on Android and iOS). When active, analytics may collect:

  • Gameplay and app events, such as session timing, onboarding steps, expedition starts and outcomes, waves and bosses reached, Endless Siege milestones, fighters recruited or upgraded, equipment crafted, route and economy choices, Ascendancy Tree progression, ad interaction events, graphics/audio settings, and non-personal bucketed progression properties
  • Device and app metadata, including device model, operating system version, app version, language/locale, and app-instance or installation identifiers
  • Approximate location derived by Google from your IP address (country, region, or city level; Briarwatch never requests precise or GPS location)
  • Diagnostics and performance metrics, including crash indicators, load times, and app stability telemetry

Google Analytics advertising-consent signals (advertising storage, advertising user data, and ad personalization) follow your regional ad consent choices, and on iOS first-party gameplay analytics operates separately from optional cross-app advertising tracking. Declining advertising tracking does not stop essential gameplay and stability measurement.

Advertising (Google Ads & Google AdMob)

Briarwatch uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob / Google Ads) to offer:

  • Optional rewarded ads — strictly player-initiated offers (such as an extra recruit reroll, a daily starting item, a King revival second chance, or bonus expedition crystals). The reward is always disclosed before you choose to watch, and declining never removes normal gameplay rewards.
  • Interstitial ads — occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks, such as after clearing a boss wave (preceded by a brief notice) or leaving an early expedition from the main menu. Interstitial ads are not shown if an ad-free entitlement is active on your profile.

Pre-release and testing builds may run in Google’s official test-ad mode during development and store review. When the Google Mobile Ads SDK initializes or requests, displays, or measures ads, Google may automatically collect or process:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general (coarse) geographic location
  • Device and advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID (when available and not deleted in your device settings) and the iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) only when you grant App Tracking Transparency permission
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, video views, reward completions, and ad clicks
  • Diagnostic and performance data related to the app and the advertising SDK

Google uses this data to deliver and cap ads, measure ad performance and attribution, prevent fraud and invalid traffic, and maintain service security. All data sent to Google is encrypted in transit using TLS/HTTPS.

Local Game Saves & Device Storage

Your active expedition, unlocked fighters, Ascendancy Tree progress, crystals, earned ad-reward receipts, and audio/graphics preferences are stored locally on your device. On Android, standard operating system backup (Android Auto Backup) may back up local app data to your personal Google account if enabled in your device settings. Briarwatch does not ask you to create a separate game account.

Optional In-App Purchases (If Offered)

If Briarwatch offers optional in-app purchases (such as an ad-free upgrade), transactions are processed solely by Google Play Billing on Android or Apple StoreKit on iOS. We never receive your payment card number or bank details. The app receives only product, transaction status, and entitlement records needed to unlock and restore your purchase, and purchase conversion events may be measured in Google Analytics.

Support & Privacy Inquiries

If you email us at ioniuncu@gmail.com, we receive your email address and any details you voluntarily send, and use them only to respond to your inquiry or fulfill your request.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses during gameplay
  • Precise or GPS location
  • Contacts, photo libraries, camera/microphone recordings, personal files, or calendar entries
  • Private messages or communications outside support emails
  • Payment-card numbers or bank account credentials
  • Health, fitness, or biometric data
  • Web browsing history outside the app

4. How We Use Information

  • App functionality & quality: Preserve local progress, diagnose crashes, optimize mobile frame pacing, and balance fighters, items, and waves.
  • Analytics: Measure how players progress through expeditions and which features need improvement.
  • Advertising & measurement: Request, display, cap, and measure ads, deliver optional ad rewards, and evaluate marketing campaigns.
  • Security & fraud prevention: Detect invalid ad traffic, protect reward integrity, and comply with store and legal requirements.

5. Third-Party Service Providers & Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. To operate analytics, advertising, and app store distribution, data may be processed by:

  • Google LLC — Google Analytics / Google Analytics for Firebase (analytics and diagnostics), Google Ads / Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK (advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention), and Google User Messaging Platform (regional consent management). Review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Platform store operators — Google Play (Android) and Apple Inc. (App Store / iOS) handle app distribution, OS-level backups, and any store-processed purchases under their own privacy policies (Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy).

These service providers may process data on servers outside your country of residence in accordance with their applicable legal safeguards and data transfer frameworks.

6. Consent, Tracking & Your Privacy Controls

  • Regional ad consent (Google UMP): Where required (including the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and applicable US states), Google’s User Messaging Platform presents a consent prompt before ads are requested. Whenever regional privacy options apply, you can reopen and change your choices anytime in-game via Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Android advertising controls: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in your device’s Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on Android version), and manage Google ad settings at Google My Ad Center.
  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS, Briarwatch will ask for your permission before allowing Google AdMob to access the IDFA for cross-app personalized advertising and attribution. Denying tracking never blocks gameplay or normal earned rewards; non-personalized or contextual ads may still be served subject to your consent choices. You can update this choice anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Optional rewarded ads: You are never required to watch a rewarded ad to play Briarwatch or keep normal gameplay rewards.
  • Deleting local data: Clearing Briarwatch’s app storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app removes your local save data and stops further collection from that installation.

7. Data Retention & Security

Local save data remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the game. Analytics and advertising data transmitted to Google are encrypted in transit and retained according to our Google Analytics / Firebase retention configuration and Google’s service policies. Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry or comply with legal obligations.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, California, and other US states with consumer privacy laws), you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we control, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of targeted advertising.

Because Briarwatch does not maintain player accounts, we cannot directly identify individual players from aggregated analytics or advertising SDK logs. You can exercise ad and tracking controls directly via the in-game Ad privacy choices screen, your Android or iOS device settings, and Google’s privacy tools. For any request concerning personal data we directly hold (such as support emails), email ioniuncu@gmail.com and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

9. Children’s Privacy

Briarwatch is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children under 13 (or the applicable digital consent age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please email ioniuncu@gmail.com so we can promptly investigate and delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy & Contact

If we materially update how Briarwatch collects or uses data — including when new analytics, advertising, or platform store features are enabled — we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date at the top.

For questions, privacy requests, or data inquiries, contact Mio/i00nqw Game Studios (Ion Iuncu) at ioniuncu@gmail.com.
