01 At the forest’s edge

A quiet clearing.

Beyond the old stone gate, the forest stirs. At your back stands the King. Between them: the army you choose to build.

Recruit fighters, equip the loot you find, and prepare your formation. Then let your army fight. Spend carefully between waves, choose your next encounter, and see how far you can hold.

When a run ends, use your earned crystals to grow the Ascendancy Tree. Return with new possibilities.
